## Why Attend Affiliate Marketing Events?

The affiliate marketing industry thrives on relationships, knowledge sharing, and staying ahead of trends. In-person events offer unparalleled opportunities to connect with potential partners, learn from industry leaders, and discover new tools and strategies.

Whether you're an affiliate publisher, advertiser, network executive, or agency professional, attending the right conferences can significantly impact your business growth. With **over 50 affiliate marketing events** scheduled worldwide in 2026, choosing where to invest your time and budget is crucial.

In this guide, we'll highlight the **must-attend events** for 2026, organized by quarter to help you plan your conference calendar effectively.

**Affiliate Summit West** remains the flagship event for the affiliate marketing industry. Held at **Caesars Forum Las Vegas**, this three-day conference brings together over **7,000 attendees** from around the world.

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Dates** | January 12-14, 2026 |
